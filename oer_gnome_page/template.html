OER-GNoME

A complete record of an autonomous DFT screening campaign for oxygen-evolution catalysts — every candidate the agent considered, every calculation it launched, and the reasoning behind each decision, including the paths it abandoned.

Candidates were drawn from the GNoME database and screened with spin-polarised RPBE+U calculations in VASP.

Electronic structure

All calculations use VASP with the projector augmented-wave method (PBE_64 potentials) and the RPBE exchange–correlation functional (GGA = RP). Every calculation is spin-polarised (ISPIN = 2) with aspherical corrections (LASPH); bulk magnetic configurations are enumerated and the lowest-energy ordering is carried forward.

A Hubbard correction is applied in Dudarev form (LDAUTYPE = 2, J = 0) to the d states of the transition metals below, following the Materials Project values:

U (eV)
Co 3.32 · Cr 3.70 · Fe 5.30 · Mn 3.90 · Mo 4.38 · Ni 6.20 · V 3.25 · W 6.20

Convergence is EDIFF = 1×10-5 eV electronically and EDIFFG = -0.05 eV/Å on forces, with Gaussian smearing (ISMEAR = 0, SIGMA = 0.1) and PREC = Accurate. k-point grids are Γ-centred and sized from the cell:

Bulk
ENCUT 520 eV · full cell relaxation (ISIF 3) · k-grid ⌈50/a⌉ × ⌈50/b⌉ × ⌈50/c⌉
Surface & adsorbate
ENCUT 400 eV · ions only, cell fixed (ISIF 0, ISYM 0) · k-grid ⌈30/a⌉ × ⌈30/b⌉ × 1

Free energies

All free energies are referenced to the computational hydrogen electrode and follow the four-electron pathway * → OH* → O* → OOH* → O₂, whose steps sum to 4.92 eV. Rungs on the diagrams are cumulative ΔG, and the highlighted riser is the potential-determining step.

Overpotentials

OOH* is never computed directly, so its rung has to be assumed. Two conventions are reported, and both diagrams and the volcano can be switched between them:

  • ηscaling uses the universal scaling relation G(OOH) = G(OH) + 3.2 eV, fixing OOH* relative to OH*.
  • ηideal drops that constraint and lets the last two steps split the remaining energy evenly — the best a catalyst could achieve if scaling were broken.

In both cases η is the largest step in the ladder minus 1.23 V. The two agree when the O/OH steps limit; where they differ, the gap is how much the scaling relation is costing that site.

Stability

max ΔGpbx is the Pourbaix decomposition energy in eV per atom, taken as the worst case across pH 0 and U in 1.2–2.0 V vs. SHE. Lower is more stable under operating conditions.

This comes from AQ-GNoME, not from the calculations on this page: AQ-GNoME's precomputed Pourbaix data is reduced to a single worst-case value over the fixed pH and potential window using its own stability criteria. The screening agent read this column when deciding which materials to pursue, alongside the disorder probabilities and HHI supply-risk scores that AQ-GNoME also supplies.
